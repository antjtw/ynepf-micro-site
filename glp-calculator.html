Your IPF GL points

To see your score, add your sex, event, kit, bodyweight and result.

How it is worked out

GL points let you compare lifters of different bodyweights. Your points are your result multiplied by a coefficient that depends on your bodyweight, sex, event and kit:

Coefficient = 100 ÷ (A − B × e^(−C × bodyweight))
IPF GL points = coefficient × result

Here e is the base of the natural logarithm, and A, B and C are fixed numbers set by the IPF for each sex, event and kit. A result of 0 scores 0 points. Scores are shown to two decimal places.

The constants A, B and C
